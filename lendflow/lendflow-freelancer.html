How It Works
Simple on both sides of the marketplace
Post once. Connect directly. Get funded — or start earning — in 24 hours.
For Lenders
1
Register & get licensed under LendFlow's Bank of Uganda framework.
2
Post your lending offer publicly — set your rate, amount range, and accepted loan types.
3
Review borrower applications. See credit scores, docs, and guarantors. Approve in one tap.
4
Collect interest payments. Track your portfolio and earnings dashboard.
For Borrowers
1
Create your profile with your NIN and phone number. Done in under 5 minutes.
2
Browse lender offers publicly, or post your own request and receive competing offers.
3
Apply or accept an offer. Upload documents and add guarantors — all on your phone.
4
Get funded in 24 hours. Money in your MTN MoMo or Airtel wallet after approval.
For Lenders
