⚠ Note on scalability — Playlists

The list+form pattern (screen 09) holds up fine for a handful of songs, but breaks down for a church that logs a full setlist history or long worship sets (10+ songs per service): the dynamic add-row form gets tall and scrolly, and the horizontal service-date cards can't preview song content. Suggested alternative for Playlists specifically: keep the horizontal service-date list up top, but tapping a card pushes into a dedicated full-screen editor (reorderable song rows, drag-to-sort, bulk-paste a setlist) instead of prefilling an inline form — the one screen in this catalogue where a push transition earns its keep over the shared pattern.
